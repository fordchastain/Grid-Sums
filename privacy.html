1. Overview

Grid Sums (“the App”) is a mobile puzzle game developed by an independent developer (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it. We respect your privacy and do not collect or store personally identifiable information (“PII”). All services related to authentication, gameplay data, and leaderboards are provided through Unity Services.

2. Information Collected and Processed

The App does not collect names, email addresses, or other direct identifiers. However, limited data may be processed through Unity Services for functionality and analytics.

Type of DataPurposeHandled By
Anonymous Player IDAuthenticate sessions and manage saved data.Unity Authentication
Leaderboard Scores & Display NamePost and display scores on leaderboards.Unity Leaderboards
Player Reports & LogsFilter profanity and maintain fair play. Only anonymous IDs are logged.Unity Cloud Code
Device InformationUsed automatically by Unity for service functionality and performance.Unity Services

We do not receive or store this data directly. All data is securely processed by Unity Technologies ApS.

3. Use of Unity Services

The App relies on the following Unity Services:

  • Unity Authentication (Anonymous Auth) – Generates a random unique ID so players can access leaderboards and cloud features without revealing personal information.
  • Unity Leaderboards – Stores gameplay scores and chosen display names.
  • Unity Cloud Code – Performs backend validation, filters profanity, and logs limited anonymous events.
For more information, review the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Future Use)

Grid Sums may include in-game advertisements provided by third-party ad networks such as Unity LevelPlay or AdMob. These services may collect limited, non-personal data such as device identifiers, region, and usage metrics to provide and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by the ad provider, not by the developer directly.

For more information, please review the relevant ad network’s privacy policy (e.g., Unity Ads Privacy Policy).

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under the age required by local law). All data processed through Unity Services is anonymous.

6. Data Retention

No personally identifiable data is stored by the developer. Anonymous gameplay records remain on Unity servers until deleted in accordance with Unity’s retention policy.

7. Data Security

All communication between the App and Unity Services occurs over HTTPS. The developer does not transmit or store any user data on independent servers.

8. Third-Party Links and Services

The App does not include external links or advertising unrelated to gameplay. Unity Services may collect aggregated analytics information as described in their own privacy documentation.

9. User Controls and Data Requests

Because the App uses anonymous authentication, there is no identifiable user data to delete. Players who wish to delete data associated with their Unity player ID should contact Unity Technologies directly using the instructions in their Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available within the App and at this public URL.
